# LINK — The Missing Link

Scroll-driven, bilingual (EN/VI) explainer of Chainlink and LINK, built around what makes Chainlink
unusual: it has no blockchain of its own. Blockchains are drawn as sealed glass blocks; Chainlink is the
chain of blue hexagon links that carries facts into them and between them.

13 chapters, each a 2D SVG scene that plays itself as you scroll: the missing link · no chain of its own ·
many nodes, one signature (OCR) · push vs pull (Feeds vs Streams) · Proof of Reserve & VRF · CCIP's two
guards (bless / curse) · the staking alarm · two flows of LINK (Reserve vs unlocks) · 2017–2022 incl. LUNA ·
banks & CRE · LINK today · strong & weak links · the chain is not the token.
