LINK, explained

A metro ride through the network that carries data onto blockchains.

How does a smart contract know what ETH costs right now?

Meet Khoa.

Khoa writes a small lending app in Saigon: you lock ETH in it and borrow dollars. To know how much to lend, the app must know the price of ETH — right now, not yesterday.

A blockchain can't look outside.

A smart contract only sees what is already on its own chain. It cannot open a website, read an exchange or call a bank. Someone has to bring the price in. That messenger is called an oracle.

If the messenger lies, everyone pays.

One wrong price lets someone borrow far too much — or wipes out honest borrowers. Chainlink is a network of many independent messengers built so that no single one can lie or switch off the feed.

Worth knowing

Chainlink says its oracles had enabled more than $36 trillion of transaction value by the third quarter of 2026.

In short

Blockchains only know what is written on them. An oracle carries outside facts in; Chainlink is the largest network of such messengers.

02Station 02 · How it works

How one price is made

Where does the number in Khoa's app come from?

Step 1: ask many sources.

Exchanges and data companies all quote ETH at slightly different prices. Chainlink does not trust any single one of them.

Step 2: many nodes, take the middle.

Many independent node operators each fetch the price. The network takes the median — the middle value — so one broken or dishonest node at the edge cannot drag the result.

Step 3: update only when it matters.

Writing to Ethereum costs fees, so the ETH/USD feed updates when the price moves 0.5% — or after one hour, whichever comes first.

Step 4: Khoa's app reads one number.

The app never talks to an exchange. It reads one on-chain number that hundreds of other apps read too.

Worth knowing

By DefiLlama's count, Chainlink secures about $31 billion across 543 protocols — far more than any other oracle.

In short

Many sources, many independent nodes, the middle value wins, and the price is refreshed when it moves 0.5% or every hour.

03Station 03 · How it works

What LINK is for

If the service is the network, what does the token do?

1 billion LINK, fixed.

All 1 billion LINK were created in 2017. 35% were sold in the token sale (about $32 million), 35% set aside for node operators and the ecosystem, and 30% kept by the company.

Staking is a deposit, not a savings account.

Node operators lock LINK as a guarantee. If a feed fails and isn't fixed in time, their stake can be cut ("slashed"). The pool holds up to 45 million LINK; the 40.875 million community share filled in about six hours in December 2023.

Usage now buys LINK.

Since August 2025 the Chainlink Reserve turns fees from its services into LINK and holds them. By 13 November 2026 it held about 6.68 million LINK, worth roughly $75 million.

But the company keeps releasing LINK.

Every quarter about 19 million LINK leave the company's "non-circulating" wallets, most of it sent to Binance. Around 748–767 million LINK now circulate.

Worth knowing

The reserve bought about 447,000 LINK in the 30 days to 5 November 2026. A quarterly unlock of ~19 million is about 6 million a month — roughly 14 times as much.

In short

LINK pays the node operators and backs them with a deposit. Usage revenue now buys LINK for a reserve, but the company still releases far more LINK than the reserve buys.

04Station 04 · How it works

Bridges between blockchains

How do tokens move from one chain to another without being stolen?

Bridges were the weakest point.

Each blockchain is an island. Bridges carry tokens between them — and became the favourite target of hackers: Ronin lost $624 million, Wormhole $325 million, Nomad $190 million, all in 2022.

Worth knowing

Chainalysis counted that 64% of all money stolen in DeFi hacks in 2022 came from bridges.

CCIP: a bridge with a second guard.

Chainlink's Cross-Chain Interoperability Protocol went live on 17 July 2023. Tokens are locked or burned on one chain and released or minted on the other, with an extra independent network checking each transfer. It now links more than 60 blockchains.

Hacks elsewhere sent traffic here.

After a $292 million exploit at KelpDAO, projects holding more than $3 billion moved to CCIP. CCIP 2.0 launched on 28 September 2026, after more than $84 billion of transfers.

In short

Bridges were crypto's weakest point. CCIP adds a separate checking network, and more projects moved to it after each hack.

05Station 05 · How it works

Banks get on the train

Why do Swift, JPMorgan and UBS work with a crypto project?

Swift: the bank messaging network.

Swift links more than 11,500 financial institutions. Its new blockchain ledger, announced in 2025, connects banks through Chainlink's software; 17 banks are piloting it.

J.P. Morgan: a test across two chains.

In May 2025 J.P. Morgan's Kinexys, Ondo Finance and Chainlink settled a tokenised Treasury fund against payment across a private and a public chain — on a test network.

UBS: a real transaction.

On 4 November 2025 UBS completed a live subscription and redemption of its tokenised money-market fund (uMINT) using Chainlink's Digital Transfer Agent standard.

Many names — read the fine print.

DTCC, Euroclear, SIX, Deutsche Börse, ANZ, Fidelity International and Mastercard also appear on Chainlink's partner list.

Worth knowing

Several of these are pilots, tests or demos. A big name on a slide is not the same as daily volume flowing through the network.

In short

Big financial players use Chainlink to connect their systems to blockchains. Many projects are still tests or pilots, not daily business.

06Station 06 · History

From a $32 million sale to $52

How did LINK get here?

2017: whitepaper and token sale.

Sergey Nazarov and Steve Ellis, with Ari Juels, published the Chainlink whitepaper in September 2017 and raised $32 million selling 350 million LINK at roughly 9–11 cents.

2019: the network goes live.

Chainlink launched on Ethereum mainnet in 2019. For nearly two years after the sale, LINK never traded above $1.30.

2020: DeFi summer.

Lending and trading apps on Ethereum boomed and all needed prices. LINK went from about $4–5 to $20.30 in August 2020 — while an anonymous "Zeus Capital" called it a scam headed for 7 cents.

Worth knowing

Fans who refused to sell called themselves "LINK Marines". A $20 million short on Aave was liquidated during the rally.

10 May 2021: the top.

LINK hit its all-time high of $52.88 — about 580 times the average sale price of 2017.

In short

Two quiet years, then DeFi made price feeds essential and LINK rose more than 500 times from its sale price.

07Station 07 · History

When a price got stuck

What happens when reality falls through the floor?

May 2022: LUNA collapses.

The Terra token LUNA fell more than 99% in days. Chainlink's LUNA feed had a built-in minimum of about $0.10 — a circuit breaker. LUNA went below it.

Stuck at $0.107, real price $0.01.

On Venus, attackers deposited 230 million LUNA worth about $2.3 million — valued by the stuck feed at ten times more — and borrowed about $13.5 million. Venus lost about $11.2 million; Blizz Finance was drained.

Who was to blame?

Chainlink said the minimum was a documented safety setting; critics said it should have reacted. The lesson for apps like Khoa's: an oracle is one piece — check its answers and have a pause button.

Worth knowing

2022 also brought bridge hacks and a bear market. Chainlink started staking in December 2022.

In short

A safety floor in the LUNA feed kept reporting $0.107 while LUNA fell to $0.01. Apps that didn't check lost millions.

08Station 08 · Today

LINK in 2026

Usage keeps growing. Why doesn't the price?

About $9, far from the top.

LINK closed at $8.85 on 15 November 2026 — about 84% below its 2021 high and 42.5% below its September 2026 peak of $15.44. Its market value is around $7 billion.

Wall Street can buy it.

Grayscale's GLNK became the first US spot Chainlink ETF on 2 December 2025; CME launched LINK futures in February 2026. US spot LINK ETFs held about $175–190 million in November 2026 — around 2% of LINK's value.

The reserve keeps growing.

About 1 million LINK in December 2025, 3.06 million at the end of March 2026, 6.05 million in September and 6.68 million on 13 November 2026.

Rivals are catching up in places.

Pyth and RedStone serve fast trading apps; RedStone has over 99% of the oracle market on Hyperliquid's EVM. Chainlink's share is estimated anywhere from about 60% to 84%, depending on who counts and how.

Busy network, weak token.

Apps can pay Chainlink in other tokens, which are converted to LINK only for the reserve. So more usage does not automatically mean more buyers of LINK.

Worth knowing

Even so, the reserve's buying is real money from real customers — something most tokens can't show.

In short

The network is busier than ever; the token trades far below its 2021 top. Usage and price are not tied tightly.

09Station 09 · Strengths & weaknesses

What Chainlink does well

Why is it the default choice?

1 · The default since 2019.

Most large DeFi lending apps, including Aave, use Chainlink prices. Being the standard is hard to take away.

2 · No single point to bribe.

Many independent nodes, a median, and staked LINK that can be slashed make one bad actor much less dangerous.

3 · Trusted by finance's plumbing.

Swift, DTCC, UBS and J.P. Morgan's Kinexys build with it — no other oracle has that list.

4 · Safer bridges.

CCIP's separate checking network attracted more than $3 billion after other bridges were hacked.

5 · Revenue that buys LINK.

The reserve turns real fees into LINK: 6.68 million LINK so far.

Five warning cards: weak link between usage and price, steady unlocks, dependence on one company, faster rivals, the LUNA lesson.

10Station 10 · Strengths & weaknesses

What can go wrong

What should a LINK holder watch?

1 · Usage ≠ price.

Fees can be paid in other tokens, and LINK is down about 84% from its high while usage grew.

2 · Steady selling from the company.

About 19 million LINK are released every quarter, mostly to Binance — roughly 14 times what the reserve buys.

3 · One company steers.

Chainlink Labs designs the products, signs the bank deals and still controls a large part of the supply.

4 · Faster rivals.

Pyth and RedStone win fast-trading and newer chains; RedStone dominates Hyperliquid's EVM.

5 · Oracles can be wrong.

The LUNA feed stuck at $0.107 in 2022 cost Venus about $11 million. No oracle removes all risk.

11Station 11 · Terminus

Conclusion

So what is LINK, in one sentence?

Chainlink is the metro system of on-chain data.

Prices, bridges and bank connections run on its tracks. It is widely used, battle-tested and hard to replace.

But the network is not the token.

A busy network does not guarantee a rising LINK price: unlocks, other payment tokens and rivals all matter.

Worth knowing

Watch three numbers: the quarterly unlocks, the reserve's buying, and how much of Chainlink's revenue is actually paid in LINK.

For Khoa — and for you.

Khoa will use Chainlink's feed, with his own safety checks. If you buy LINK, know you are betting on the token, not only on the network. As of November 2026 Vietnam has no licensed crypto exchange yet. This is education, not investment advice.
