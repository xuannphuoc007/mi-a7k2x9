BNB & Binance, explained

When an app says you own bitcoin, who actually has it?

No crypto knowledge needed. We start with $100.

Minh is a student in Hanoi. He wants to buy $100 of bitcoin, but his bank doesn't sell it. Like most people, he downloads an exchange app. Scroll down — the picture tells the story.

An exchange is two businesses in one.

A marketplace: it matches Minh with someone who wants to sell. A vault: after the trade, it keeps the bitcoin for him.

Minh's app shows "0.0014 BTC". That number is a line in the exchange's own records — a promise that it holds the coin for him.

The biggest exchange of them all is Binance.

Binance says it is closing in on 300 million users and it is the largest crypto exchange by trading volume. Its own coin, BNB, is one of the biggest cryptocurrencies.

Binance at Binance Blockchain Week, Dec 2025 (The Block).

So its story affects millions of people.

Binance grew faster than any exchange in history. It also pleaded guilty to U.S. crimes, saw its founder go to prison and then be pardoned, faces new allegations it denies, and suffered the largest crypto theft on record.

This story covers both: how it works, and what it has been accused of — with dates and sources.

In short

A crypto exchange is a marketplace and a vault at the same time. Your app balance is the exchange's promise — which is why who runs the exchange matters so much.

A timeline from Changpeng Zhao's trading-software career to Binance's July 2017 launch, funded by selling 100 million BNB tokens, then a move out of China.

02Act 02 · Origin

The founder and a $15M start

QHow did a 2017 start-up become the world's biggest exchange in months?

Changpeng Zhao — "CZ".

Born in China in 1977, he moved to Canada as a teenager and spent his early career building trading software for stock exchanges and Bloomberg. He discovered bitcoin in 2013 and worked briefly at the Chinese exchange OKCoin.

July 2017: Binance opens, funded by its own coin.

CZ and co-founder Yi He raised about $15 million in an online token sale, selling BNB for roughly 10–15 cents each. Of the 200 million BNB created, half went to the public, 40% to the founding team and 10% to early investors. (A 2023 Forbes investigation, using on-chain data, argued the sale really raised under $5 million and that far fewer tokens reached buyers; Binance has stood by its figures.)

CoinDesk; BNB whitepaper (2017).

September 2017: China bans exchanges — Binance moves.

Weeks after launch, China shut down local crypto exchanges. Binance shifted to Japan, then Malta and elsewhere. For years it had no official headquarters — a strength for growth, and a weakness with regulators.

Within months, the largest exchange in the world.

Low fees, hundreds of coins and a fast app pulled in traders. BNB became the coin that gave discounts on those fees — tying its value to Binance's success.

In short

Binance started in 2017 with about $15 million raised by selling its own BNB token. It grew at record speed — partly by moving fast across borders, which later became its biggest legal problem.

03Act 03 · The journey

Nine years in fast-forward

QWhat has happened to Binance and BNB since 2017?

2017–2019: launch, China ban, first big hack.

In May 2019 hackers stole 7,000 bitcoin (about $40 million then). Binance covered it from an emergency fund it had set aside.

2020–2022: its own blockchain, a bridge hack, FTX's collapse.

BNB Smart Chain launched in 2020. In October 2022 a bridge on that chain was exploited for about $570 million, and validators halted the chain. A month later rival exchange FTX collapsed — and Binance became even more dominant.

2023–2024: guilty plea and prison.

In November 2023 Binance and CZ pleaded guilty to U.S. charges and agreed to pay $4.3 billion. CZ stepped down and served four months in prison in 2024.

2025: political turn, record price, a pardon.

A $2 billion investment settled in a Trump-family-linked stablecoin, the SEC dropping its case, the 10/10 crash, a record BNB price — and in October, a presidential pardon for CZ.

2026: new allegations and the largest hack ever.

Reports of Iran-linked flows and new probes, which Binance denies and is suing over. Then on 29 October, about $6.3 billion stolen. By mid-November BNB was near $600.

Prices: Coin Metrics daily closes to 23 May 2026, then dated press reports (dashed).

In short

BNB went from about 10 cents to a record near $1,375 in October 2025. Along the way came hacks, a criminal guilty plea, a pardon and the biggest crypto theft ever. The price has followed both the market and the headlines.

An order book: buyers' prices on one side, sellers' prices on the other. Orders meet in the middle, trades print, and a small fee drops into the exchange's account.

04Act 04 · How it works

The marketplace

QWhat happens when Minh presses 'Buy'?

Buyers on one side, sellers on the other.

Every buyer posts the highest price they'll pay; every seller the lowest they'll accept. The list is called the order book. When the prices meet, the exchange's computer matches them — thousands of times a second.

On every trade, the exchange earns a fee.

A standard spot trade on Binance costs about 0.1%. Pay that fee in BNB and you get a discount — one of the main reasons people hold BNB.

Multiply tiny fees by billions of dollars of trading a day and you get one of the most profitable companies in crypto.

After the trade, who holds the bitcoin?

Binance does. Most coins sit in offline 'cold' wallets; some sit in online 'hot' wallets so withdrawals are fast. Minh's balance is a number in Binance's database.

That's convenient — and it means Minh trusts Binance's security, honesty and ability to pay. Crypto users have a saying: "not your keys, not your coins."

In short

The exchange matches buyers and sellers and takes a small fee on every trade. After the trade, your coins usually stay in the exchange's wallets — not yours.

05Act 04 · How it works

Proof of reserves

QHow can you tell an exchange really has your money?

November 2022: FTX shows what can go wrong.

FTX, then one of the largest exchanges, had quietly used customers' money. When people rushed to withdraw, it couldn't pay and collapsed within days. Every exchange was suddenly asked: do you really hold our coins?

Binance's answer: a public proof.

Binance began publishing 'proof of reserves'. All customer balances are hashed into a tree; you can check your own balance is in it. Binance then shows its wallets hold at least as much of each coin as customers are owed.

›Go deeper

The tree is called a Merkle tree: each pair of balances is combined into one fingerprint, then pairs of fingerprints, up to a single 'root'. Change any balance and the root changes.

What it can't show.

A snapshot doesn't show debts the company owes elsewhere, whether coins were borrowed just for the snapshot, or how safe the wallets are. An accounting firm that had reviewed Binance's first report stopped working with crypto clients in December 2022.

After the October 2026 hack, Binance expanded the proof to 44 tokens.

CoinGabbar on the 4 Nov 2026 proof-of-reserves snapshot.

In short

Proof of reserves lets you check your balance is counted and that the exchange holds at least that much of each coin. It is not a full audit: debts and other liabilities can stay out of view.

06Act 04 · How it works

BNB: the exchange's own coin

QWhat is BNB for, why is its supply shrinking — and why do meme coins move it?

Three main jobs.

A discount coupon for trading fees on Binance. The 'gas' that pays for transactions on BNB Chain. And a ticket to Binance's token-launch programmes.

Burning towards 100 million.

BNB started with 200 million coins. Every quarter a formula-based 'auto-burn' destroys some, plus part of every fee on BNB Chain. After the 37th burn in October 2026, about 131.5 million remained, against a target of 100 million.

BNB Chain / TokenPost via CoinPaprika, Nov 2026.

The catch: one company in the middle.

Unlike bitcoin or ether, BNB was created by a company that sold it to fund itself, kept 40% for its team and still drives most of its use. Good news for Binance tends to lift BNB; bad news hits it directly.

Meme coins: the casino on BNB Chain.

Anyone can launch a token on BNB Chain for a few dollars through launchpads such as Four.meme. In February 2025 CZ posted a photo of his dog and its name, Broccoli. Within hours hundreds of copycat 'Broccoli' tokens appeared — at least 300 on Four.meme and more than 480 on Solana. CZ said he was not issuing a coin himself: it was "up to the community".

The Block; Cointelegraph, 13 Feb 2025.

›Go deeper

A week earlier, TST — a 'test' token BNB Chain staff created for a how-to video — had turned into a real meme coin.

October 2025: "BNB meme szn".

As BNB set records above $1,300, CZ posted "#BNB meme szn!". The leader was a token called '4' — named after his habit of replying '4' to criticism, meaning 'ignore the FUD' — which passed $240 million in value. Chinese-named tokens such as 币安人生 ('Binance Life') rose by thousands of percent, and Four.meme briefly out-earned Solana's Pump.fun.

Decrypt; Forklog; Cointelegraph, Oct 2025.

Who actually wins?

The pattern repeats: early wallets buy cheap, hype spreads, late buyers arrive near the top, insiders sell. One Broccoli token's creator reportedly sold his whole stack within 20 minutes for about $6.5 million. In October an on-chain analyst claimed single wallets held about 39% of PALU, 23% of 'Binance Life' and 14% of '4' at points — unverified claims — and many of these coins fell around 30% within days.

KuCoin News; Cointelegraph (Oct 2025).

In short

BNB gives discounts and pays fees on BNB Chain, and Binance burns part of the supply every quarter. That makes BNB unusually tied to one company's fortunes — and, in meme seasons, to a single founder's posts.

Forty-five validators on BNB Chain beside the crowd of validators on Ethereum; blocks fly by every 0.45 seconds; in 2022 the validators are contacted one by one and the chain stops.

07Act 04 · How it works

BNB Chain: fast, but few

QIs BNB Chain decentralised — and what do people actually do on it?

A copy of Ethereum, tuned for speed.

BNB Chain runs software forked from Ethereum, so the same apps work. But instead of hundreds of thousands of validators, it uses 45 active ones — 21 at a time — and produces a block about every 0.45 seconds.

BNB Chain docs and BNBScan via CoinPaprika, 5 Nov 2026.

October 2022: the chain that could be stopped.

When a bridge was hacked for about $570 million, BNB Chain contacted its validators "one by one" and halted the network within hours, limiting the damage. On Ethereum or Bitcoin, that kind of quick stop is practically impossible.

Two ways to read it.

Supporters: fast, cheap, and able to protect users in an emergency. Critics: a few parties — many close to Binance — can coordinate to change or stop the chain. One analysis puts the number needed to stall it at about 7, versus about 30 for Ethereum. BNB Chain says Binance does not control BNB or the chain.

Blockworks (2022); QuickNode on validator concentration.

DeFi: bank-like apps with no bank.

Most activity on BNB Chain is 'DeFi' — finance run by code. On PancakeSwap, the biggest app, there is no seller on the other side: Minh trades against a pool holding two tokens, and the price shifts as the pool's balance changes. Venus lets people deposit coins as collateral and borrow against them; Aster offers futures with high leverage.

Messari, Q4 2025: PancakeSwap, Lista DAO and Venus were the largest apps.

Busy, but smaller than it looks.

DefiLlama counted about $4.9 billion deposited in BNB Chain apps on 12 November 2026 — around Solana's level, but roughly a tenth of Ethereum's ~$50 billion. BNB Chain often leads in daily users and trades because fees are tiny.

DefiLlama; CryptoBriefing (2026).

›Go deeper

In October 2025 DefiLlama stopped showing Aster's futures volume, saying it mirrored Binance's almost one-to-one and could not be checked for wash trading. Supporters said Binance liquidity had simply moved on-chain. Aster is advised by CZ and backed by YZi Labs, formerly Binance Labs.

The dark side of cheap trades.

Bots watch for pending swaps, buy just before and sell just after — a 'sandwich' that makes the victim pay more. In late 2024 a record of more than a third of BNB Chain blocks contained one. BNB Chain says a validator alliance launched in 2025 cut such attacks by about 95%, though waves returned. Immunefi has counted about $1.6 billion lost to hacks and rug pulls on the chain since 2017; HashDit counted $61 million across 120 incidents in 2025.

Decrypt; BNB Chain; Immunefi; HashDit.

In short

BNB Chain is fast and cheap because only a small set of validators run it. That same smallness let it be paused within hours in 2022 — useful in a crisis, and the main reason critics call it centralised. On top run swaps, loans and futures: cheap and fast, with far less money than Ethereum and plenty of bots and scams.

A $4.3 billion stack splits into a criminal fine and forfeited money; CZ's name moves from CEO to a four-month prison term; a monitor watches the company.

08Act 05 · Accusations

Guilty: the 2023 plea

QWhat exactly did Binance and CZ admit?

21 November 2023: guilty pleas in Seattle.

Binance admitted failing to keep an effective anti-money-laundering programme, running an unlicensed money-transmitting business and violating U.S. sanctions. It agreed to pay $4.3 billion — a $1.8 billion criminal fine plus $2.5 billion forfeited — and to accept an independent monitor.

U.S. Department of Justice; Shearman & Sterling analysis.

What officials said it allowed.

U.S. officials said the failures let money connected to child abuse, drug trafficking and terrorism move through the exchange, and that Binance served users in sanctioned places such as Iran.

Binance said the settlement let it "turn the page" and invest heavily in compliance.

AP; Treasury and DOJ statements, Nov 2023.

CZ: resignation, $50 million, four months.

CZ pleaded guilty personally to a Bank Secrecy Act violation, paid a $50 million fine and stepped down as CEO. Richard Teng took over. In April 2024 a judge sentenced CZ to four months in prison; he was released in September 2024.

In short

This part is not an allegation: Binance and CZ pleaded guilty in a U.S. court. The crimes were about failing to stop and report illegal money — not about stealing customers' funds.

Three dated events line up — a $2 billion investment settled in a Trump-family-linked stablecoin, the SEC dropping its case, a presidential pardon — with supporters' and critics' readings on either side.

09Act 05 · Accusations

Politics and a pardon

QWhy do critics link Binance's comeback to U.S. politics?

2025: three things happen in seven months.

March–May: Abu Dhabi's MGX invests $2 billion in Binance, settled in USD1 — a stablecoin from the Trump family's World Liberty Financial. 29 May: the SEC drops its 2023 civil case against Binance and CZ, with prejudice. 23 October: President Trump pardons CZ.

The Block; Reuters; SEC litigation release, 29 May 2025.

The case for the pardon.

The White House said CZ was prosecuted in the previous administration's "war on crypto", with no allegations of fraud or identifiable victims. Supporters note a prison term was unusual for a first-time Bank Secrecy Act case. President Trump said he did not know CZ.

The case against.

Critics, including Senator Elizabeth Warren, said CZ pleaded guilty, then boosted one of the President's crypto ventures and lobbied for a pardon. By February 2026 Binance reportedly held about 89% of all USD1 in custody — deepening concerns about ties between the exchange and the President's family business.

Reuters; DL News (Feb 2026).

In short

Facts: a Trump-linked stablecoin was used for a $2 billion Binance deal, the SEC dropped its case, and CZ was pardoned. Supporters call it the end of an unfair crackdown; critics call it a conflict of interest. Both readings are below.

Two cases side by side. The 10/10 crash: prices on Binance briefly break, then $283 million is repaid. The 2026 Iran-flow reports: a newspaper report, Senate letters, a reported Justice Department probe — and Binance's denial and lawsuit.

10Act 05 · Accusations

Open allegations & market questions

QWhat is Binance accused of now — including moving prices — and what does it say?

10 October 2025: the biggest liquidation day ever.

Over $19 billion of leveraged bets were wiped out across crypto. On Binance, the dollar token USDe briefly traded near $0.65 and other collateral lost its peg, liquidating users. Binance blamed technical glitches and market conditions, said the depegs came after the market bottom, and repaid about $283 million.

CoinGecko; The Block; Binance statement, Oct 2025.

February 2026: reports of Iran-linked flows.

The Wall Street Journal reported that Binance's own investigators had traced about $1.7 billion to Iran-linked entities and were later dismissed. Senator Richard Blumenthal opened an inquiry; 11 senators asked Treasury and the Justice Department to investigate; the Journal later reported a Justice Department probe.

Decrypt; Cointelegraph; Senate letter, 24 Feb 2026.

Binance's answer: "defamatory".

Binance told the Senate its review found no accounts transacting directly with Iranian entities, said it did not fire staff for raising concerns, and on 11 March 2026 sued the Journal's publisher for defamation. The Journal stands by its reporting and has asked the court to dismiss the suit. No court has ruled on the facts.

The Block; Courthouse News via Talking Biz News (Aug 2026).

What 'manipulation' means.

Wash trading: trading with yourself to make a coin look busy. Front-running: buying before news you know is coming. Dumping: a big holder selling into everyone else's buy orders. In stock markets these are crimes; in crypto the rules are patchier.

The SEC's 2023 lawsuit alleged that a CZ-controlled firm, Sigma Chain, wash-traded on Binance.US and that customer money was mixed at another CZ firm, Merit Peak. Binance denied it. The SEC dropped the case in 2025, so no court ever ruled on those claims.

SEC press release 2023-101; Reuters.

Cases Binance itself confirmed.

In March 2025 Binance banned a market maker that had sold about 66 million MOVE tokens the day after listing, with few buy orders, netting $38 million; it froze the proceeds. That month, and again in December 2025, it suspended employees for trading on inside information about token launches.

Cointelegraph; The Block; Binance announcements.

Claims Binance disputes.

A fund boss claimed in 2024 that Binance asked a project for 15% of its tokens to list it; co-founder Yi He denied it, saying listing fees are voluntary donations to charity. After the 10/10 crash, critics — including the CEO of rival exchange OKX — blamed Binance's pricing system and promotions. Binance admitted a pricing-feed glitch and repaid users; CZ called claims that Binance caused the crash "far-fetched".

The Defiant; Cointelegraph; Bloomberg via Yahoo Finance.

In short

These are allegations, not findings. Binance denies the Iran-flow reports and is suing the Wall Street Journal; the newspaper stands by its reporting. Watch how the investigations and the lawsuit end. On market abuse, some cases were confirmed and punished by Binance itself; others were dropped, denied or remain disputed. No court has found Binance guilty of market manipulation.

Hot wallets on about ten blockchains empty within hours; a frozen portion is held back by partners; a replacement stack refills customers' balances.

11Act 05 · Accusations

The $6.3 billion hack

QWhat happened on 29 October 2026?

About $6.3 billion, in one evening.

From 20:05 UTC on 29 October, Binance's online 'hot' wallets on about ten blockchains were drained — more than four times the 2025 Bybit theft, until then the record. Blockchain analysts linked it to North Korean hackers; Binance and governments had not confirmed that at the time.

TRM Labs; Chainalysis via Al Jazeera; Binance incident update.

Freeze, pause, repay.

Stablecoin issuers and partners froze about $3 billion — Tether alone over 1.5 billion USDT. Binance paused withdrawals and reopened them coin by coin from 1 November. It said the attack "should not have happened" and that it would bear the full cost; by 13 November it said it had covered more than $6.34 billion with company funds.

What it means.

Customers were made whole — something only a very large company could afford. But withdrawals were frozen for days, and BNB fell about 23% in the two weeks after. Critics ask why so much sat in online wallets; Binance has promised an independent security review.

Memeburn; CryptoTicker, Nov 2026.

In short

The largest crypto theft on record hit Binance's hot wallets, not customers' balances: Binance covered the losses with company money. It showed both Binance's size and the risk of keeping coins on any exchange.

12Act 06 · People & reality

The people at the top

QWho runs Binance now — and why does CZ divide the crypto world?

CZ — the founder.

Pleaded guilty in 2023, served four months, pardoned in 2025. He says he no longer manages the exchange, but remains Binance's best-known figure and a constant voice on X. After the pardon, The Block noted it could pave the way for his return.

Yi He — co-founder, co-CEO since December 2025.

Ran marketing and customer service from the start. Named co-CEO on 3 December 2025 alongside Richard Teng. She is CZ's life partner; CZ wrote she "should have been the Binance CEO from day 1". Critics see the appointment as founder control continuing.

The Block; Banking Dive, Dec 2025.

Richard Teng — the ex-regulator.

A former financial regulator in Singapore and Abu Dhabi, CEO since November 2023, now co-CEO. He is the face of Binance's compliance push — and the one answering the 2026 Senate letters and leading the response to the hack.

CZ: from hero to lightning rod.

In 2019, after a hack, he reassured users that funds were 'SAFU' — Binance paid the losses and the phrase became a meme. In 2022 his public sale of FTX's token helped set off the run that exposed FTX's fraud; FTX later accused Binance of speeding its fall. Then came the 2023 guilty plea and four months in prison. His raised-four-fingers photo — '4' means 'ignore the FUD' — became a meme of its own.

Cointelegraph; Yahoo Finance.

2025–2026: the backlash.

After the pardon, CZ posted constantly — dog photos, 'meme season', predictions of a 'supercycle'. Each post moved coins. In January 2026 traders turned on him and Binance over 'Alpha' listings: community-compiled data suggested more than 90% of 221 projects listed in 2025–26 were trading far below their post-listing highs. Viral posts demanded he be 'sent back to jail'.

CCN, 27 Jan 2026; Crypto Times, Jan 2026.

Two readings of CZ.

Supporters: he holds no role at Binance, paid his penalty, users were repaid after each incident, and he can't control what strangers launch. Critics: as the biggest shareholder he still shapes Binance; his posts move coins he says he doesn't trade; and in November 2026 OKX's CEO again blamed him and Binance for two years of crashes — which CZ denies.

Protos; Blockzeit; Our Crypto Talk, Nov 2026.

In short

Binance is led by a former regulator and a co-founder, while its pardoned founder remains its most influential voice. Critics say the founder's circle still holds the real power; Binance says the two-CEO model combines compliance and innovation.

13Act 06 · People & reality

On the exchange or in your wallet?

QWhere should Minh keep his $100 of bitcoin?

Two ways to hold the same coin.

Keep it on the exchange, or withdraw it to a wallet only you control. The table below compares them.

A simple rule many people use.

Keep on an exchange only what you are actively trading; move long-term savings to a wallet you control and back up the recovery phrase offline. Either way, never share that phrase with anyone.

In short

An exchange is convenient and can make you whole after a hack — if it chooses and is able to. Your own wallet removes that dependence but puts all the responsibility on you.

14Act 06 · People & reality

Reality check

QStrengths, risks — and how BNB compares with Bitcoin and Ethereum.

What's strong.

The most users and trading volume in crypto. It has repaid users after the 2019 hack, the 10/10 crash and the 2026 theft. Proof of reserves is public. BNB's supply keeps shrinking through burns.

What's risky.

A 2023 criminal conviction and ongoing monitoring; new allegations it denies; close ties to U.S. political interests that critics question; the largest hack on record; and a coin and chain that depend heavily on one company.

Both are true at once.

BNB hit a record near $1,375 in October 2025 and was near $600 in mid-November 2026 — down more than half. If you hold BNB, you are betting on Binance itself: its business, its legal fights and its security.

AMBCrypto (record high); ad-hoc-news, 14 Nov 2026.

Bitcoin vs Ethereum vs BNB.

Bitcoin has no company behind it. Ethereum is steered by a non-profit foundation and many independent teams. BNB was created by Binance, and its chain still depends on Binance's world.

Different rules, different speeds.

Bitcoin's supply stops at 21 million; Ethereum has no fixed cap; BNB burns toward 100 million. A Bitcoin block takes about 10 minutes, Ethereum's 12 seconds, BNB Chain's under half a second — speed bought with far fewer validators.

Size.

In mid-November 2026 Bitcoin was worth about $1.4 trillion, Ethereum about $240 billion and BNB about $77 billion. The table below sums up the differences.

In short

Binance is the biggest, most liquid exchange and has repaid users after every major incident. It is also a convicted company with a pardoned founder, open allegations and the record hack. BNB carries all of that.

Back to Minh

QSo — what should Minh do with his $100?

Minh bought his bitcoin in seconds.

The order matched, the fee was a few cents, and his app shows the balance. Now he knows that balance is Binance's promise, backed by Binance's reserves, security and record.

What he decides.

He keeps a small amount on the exchange to trade, withdraws the rest to his own wallet, writes the recovery phrase on paper — and keeps an eye on the news in this story.
